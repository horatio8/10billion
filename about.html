§ About / What we believe

An operator-run shop.
Not an agency in the traditional sense.

The Ten Billion Agency was started by the operator who built a personal content portfolio that crossed ten billion organic views. The thesis: most agencies sell strategy. We sell output. If your numbers don't move, neither does our reputation.

§ Principles / Four things we hold

How we operate.

01

Hooks before everything.

If the first 1.4 seconds don't earn the next ten, nothing downstream matters. We rebuild every content engine around a tested hook library before we touch anything else.

02

Volume is a tool, not a goal.

Most brands ship too much content of the wrong kind. We routinely cut output by 30–60% and watch lift accelerate. Cadence serves the hook, not the other way around.

03

Operators talk to operators.

No account managers, no relationship layers, no juniors. You work directly with the strategist who is going to do the thinking. If we can't staff that way, we don't take the engagement.

04

Receipts beat decks.

Every claim on this site is backed by platform-verified analytics. Every monthly report ties to the same source of truth. If a number can't be verified, we don't put it in writing.

§ Origin / How this started

The short version.

In 2022, our founder started a single short-form video account. No team, no agency, no budget. Inside 18 months it crossed a billion views. Inside three years, ten billion.

Along the way, the methodology — a hook-first, evidence-driven, ruthlessly tested approach to short-form content — got reverse-engineered into a system. Founders started asking to license it. DTC brands started paying for advisory time.

By the end of 2025 we had quietly run six full content engines under a private retainer model. The Ten Billion Agency is the public-facing version of what that team has been doing for a while.

We're based in Charleston, with satellite operators in NYC and LA. We take on a deliberately small slate — never more than nine active retainers at once — because the methodology only works when the operator is the one operating it.
